About Me

I'm a filmmaker based in Toronto working in the grip and electric department, and as a 2nd AC on narrative, commercial, and music video productions. I document my journey through 35mm film photography, capturing candid moments from set life and the world around me. With hands-on experience on dozens of sets and a strong visual eye, I aspire to evolve into a Director of Photography and craft compelling visual narratives.

I studied film at Toronto Film School and Yorkville University, and have collaborated with passionate crews across Ontario's film scene.
